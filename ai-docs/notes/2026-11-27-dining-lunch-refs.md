# Dining room and lunch room refs (session 41)

Source: the PRR booklet "Pennsylvania station in New York city" (1910, archive.org pennsylvaniastat00penn, not in copyright). Three plates saved in refs/photos, logged in refs/SOURCES.md.

## Where the rooms sit (booklet text)
The arcade (225 x 45 ft) "expands into the Loggia, a colonnaded hall, affording entrance to the dining room and cafe on one side, and the luncheon room and buffet on the other. A broad stone stairway leads directly into the general waiting room." The Cassatt statue stands in a niche in the Loggia. The kitchens are on the floor above. So both rooms open off the stair hall at the landing level (z 192 in the map), one north and one south. The map's stair hall is the Loggia.

## What the plates show
- Restaurant and dining room (n19): a long rectangular hall, flat ceiling divided by deep beams into large coffered panels, a row of tall round-headed windows along one long side, brass chandeliers hanging down the centre line, square tables with white cloths and bentwood chairs filling the floor, panelled walls with a dado.
- Loggia between the restaurant and lunch room (n20): coffered barrel vault, columns on pedestals, a large round-headed window at the end, openings to each side. Matches what the map already has for the stair hall.
- Lunch room and buffet (n24): a lower hall, flat coffered ceiling on beams, pendant lamps in rows, long counters with stools running the length of the room, a mirrored/panelled back wall.

## Rulings (flagged, small)
- Dining room on the north side of the Loggia, lunch room on the south side (the booklet does not say which side; the 1910 waiting-room level plan in refs should confirm it at blockout).
- Size: about 100 x 60 ft each, ceiling about 35 ft, until the plan is measured.
- Tables, chairs, counters and chandeliers as MDL props (vertex budget), the coffered ceilings as texture.
