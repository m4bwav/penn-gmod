# Exterior refs (session 35)

Seven public-domain refs for area 5, logged in refs/SOURCES.md. What they show, for the blockout:

- **Seventh Avenue (east) front** (HABS EAST_FACADE, Old_Penn_Station_NY_exterior, Detroit postcard, booklet 14573312397): one long Doric colonnade on a low stepped base. The central pavilion over the arcade entrance rises higher, with a plain attic carrying the clock flanked by two reclining figures and eagles at the corners. Each end of the front turns the corner as a projecting pavilion with a pediment (temple front) on the side streets. Cast-iron lamp standards along the kerb.
- **31st Street (south) front** (SOUTH_FACADE_FROM_SOUTHEAST, booklet 14573132588): long ashlar wall with recessed panels and small square windows near the street. The carriage-drive entrance is a second Doric colonnade mid-block; above it the waiting room's thermal window shows in a tall arched gable that rises over the roofline. Balustrade along the top of the low wings.
- **West end of the south front** (WEST_END_OF_SOUTH_FACADE): the same colonnade-and-pavilion scheme seen from the west, with eagle groups on the pavilion attic.
- The 33rd Street (north) front mirrors the south one (plans in refs/photos already show the symmetry).
