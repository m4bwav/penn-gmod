# Era: the original 1910 station

Date: 2026-11-26 (session 1). Ruling made by the agent under the chain prompt's default; flagged to Mark.

Build the 1910 McKim, Mead & White Pennsylvania Station (demolished 1963-66), not the present-day station.

Why:
- Public-domain references exist: HABS/HAER drawings and photos at the Library of Congress, Detroit Publishing Co. photos, pre-1929 postcards. The present station is mostly copyrighted photos.
- The 1910 interiors (waiting room after the Baths of Caracalla, steel-and-glass concourse) are the reason people want to walk it; the current station is low basement halls.
- Grand Central's map showed the tooling can carry a big classical hall within vbsp caps.

The present-day station and Moynihan Train Hall stay an optional extra (area 6) if the budget allows.
