## Sources

Every reference is public domain or has no known restrictions: the HABS photographs by Cervin Robinson (1962), Library of Congress prints, Joseph Pennell's etchings, the McKim, Mead & White floor plans, and the Pennsylvania Railroad's own 1910 booklets. The full list with links is [refs/SOURCES.md](https://github.com/m4bwav/penn-gmod/blob/main/refs/SOURCES.md). Photos are used for proportions and colour only; no photo is in the map.

## What is measured and what is estimated

- From published dimensions and the plans: the waiting room (314 x 109 ft, 150 ft to the vault crown), the arcade (225 ft), the Seventh Avenue front (430 ft), the concourse and the stair positions.
- Read from photos: column heights and spacing, arch and window sizes, cornice heights, the clock group.
- Estimated: the dining room and lunch room sizes (one view each in the 1910 booklet), the side-street depth, and the travertine colour, judged by eye from the photos.

## Known simplifications

- The real station had 21 tracks on 11 platforms. The map has one island platform under each concourse stair, a single track between neighbours, and 500 ft of platform to the tunnel portals.
- Interior figures, signage and furniture are sparse.
- Stock Half-Life 2 materials fill in where no custom texture exists yet; they ship with Garry's Mod and aren't bundled.
