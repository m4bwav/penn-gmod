[h1]Pennsylvania Station, 1910[/h1]
The lost Pennsylvania Station in New York, as McKim, Mead & White built it in 1910 and as it stood until 1963, at true scale. Walk in from Seventh Avenue through the Doric colonnade, down the 225 ft shopping arcade with its bronze shopfronts and lamp standards, past the Cassatt statue and down the grand stair into the main waiting room: three groin-vaulted bays 150 ft high, coffered vaults, giant Corinthian columns and thermal windows with morning sun on the floor. West of the waiting room is the steel-and-glass train concourse: glass groin vaults on branching lattice columns, a clock over the travertine arcade, and stairs down toward the tracks.

The track platforms and a train will follow in updates.
