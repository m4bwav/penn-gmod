[h1]Pennsylvania Station, 1910[/h1]
The lost Pennsylvania Station in New York, as McKim, Mead & White built it in 1910 and as it stood until 1963, at true scale. This first release is the main waiting room: three groin-vaulted bays 150 ft high, coffered vaults, giant Corinthian columns, thermal windows with morning sun on the floor, and the grand stair up to the arcade.

The arcade, the steel-and-glass train concourse, the platforms and the Seventh Avenue colonnade will follow in updates.
